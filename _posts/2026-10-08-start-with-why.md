I've seen too many engineers waste hours because they didn't stop to ask one question:

**Why?**

- Why does the customer need this feature?
- Why migrate to this technology?
- Why should we separate this into a microservice?
- Why do we need to fix this security scan finding?

It's easy to get caught up in hype or implement a solution just because it's cool to work on.

We're engineers. Problem solvers. Craftsmen.

The moment we blindly execute, we're doing work that can be handed to an AI agent. And without the "why," we risk wasting time and effort solving the wrong problem.

**Don't just build what you're asked to build. Understand why you're building it.**
