True seniority comes from three things:

**Realizing that you don't know everything.** Honestly, neither does anyone else.

**Being humble.** Recognize every opportunity to learn from someone, no matter their title.

**Being confident in your experience.** You've solved enough problems to understand that uncertainty is a challenge waiting to be solved.
